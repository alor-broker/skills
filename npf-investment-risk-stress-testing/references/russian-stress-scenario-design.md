# Russian stress-scenario design

Design scenarios around mechanisms relevant to the Russian market:

- parallel and non-parallel OFZ curve moves;
- RUONIA and funding-spread changes;
- corporate-spread widening, rating migration, default, and recovery uncertainty;
- equity drawdown with volatility and correlation shifts;
- ruble moves and restrictions on convertibility, settlement, or custody;
- commodity-price and export-flow shocks;
- inflation and fiscal-policy surprises;
- market closure, stale prices, reduced depth, wider bid-ask spreads, and delayed settlement;
- combined shocks and second-order feedback.

Historical periods such as 1998, 2008, 2014–2016, 2020, and 2022 represent different structures. Use them only after checking instrument availability, trading conditions, index construction, frozen observations, survivorship, and regime differences. Never mechanically rescale one episode into a probability forecast.
