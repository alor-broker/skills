# NPF Issuer Investment Analysis

Prepare an issuer and instrument review for pension-asset decisions. Cover business quality, financial statements, cash generation, leverage, refinancing, credit, instrument terms, governance, conflicts, liquidity, valuation, and thesis risks.

## Work with the available context

Use disclosures, market data, research, models, and transaction context already supplied or obtained through authorized systems. Do not prompt for holdings, planned orders, target weights, counterparties, term sheets, allocations, internal ratings, limits, committee papers, broker statements, account identifiers, or confidential issuer information. If a decisive item is absent, expose the dependency and keep the conclusion conditional rather than asking for disclosure.

Classify evidence as public, user-supplied, internal, third-party, estimated, or assumed. Keep organization-specific facts separate from external verification. Do not place such facts in external queries or third-party tools without explicit authorization for the destination and purpose.

## Establish the case

Identify the legal issuer, operating group, security, seniority, currency, governing terms, analysis date, horizon, and evidence cutoff. Read [references/issuer-source-hierarchy.md](references/issuer-source-hierarchy.md) before research, [references/issuer-analysis-framework.md](references/issuer-analysis-framework.md) for the company review, and [references/instrument-specific-checks.md](references/instrument-specific-checks.md) for the security.

Prefer audited statements, notes, official disclosures, prospectuses, offer documents, exchange records, rating-agency publications, official registers, and identified supplied sources. Verify material external claims against the original source where possible.

Reconcile IFRS, RAS, and other perimeters rather than mixing them. Separate reported facts, management claims, internal views, rating opinions, market prices, estimates, assumptions, and calculations. Analyze profit quality, operating cash conversion, working capital, capex, debt maturity, liquidity sources, disclosed off-balance-sheet exposures, related parties, governance, state relationships, sanctions and infrastructure dependencies, and instrument liquidity.

Build base, adverse, and falsification cases. Do not equate state ownership with a legal guarantee. Construct the strongest objection a credit analyst, equity analyst, risk officer, or skeptical committee member would make and reflect unresolved issues in the recommendation.

## Output

Return the thesis, confidence, issuer and instrument identity, source and date table, financial reconciliation, credit and cash-flow analysis, instrument terms, valuation with formulas, scenarios, conflict indicators, strongest objection, thesis breakers, missing dependencies, recommendation, and conditions for review.

Distinguish investment analysis from asset eligibility, legal compliance, internal limit approval, and order authorization. Respond in the user's language with equivalent safeguards.
