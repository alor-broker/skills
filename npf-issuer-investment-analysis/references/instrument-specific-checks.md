# Instrument-specific checks

## Bonds

Verify issuer, guarantor, seniority, security, currency, nominal, coupon, floating-rate formula, amortization, maturity, offers, calls, covenants, events of default, settlement, listing, accrued interest, clean or dirty price, yield convention, duration, liquidity, ratings, and use of proceeds. Distinguish issuer credit from instrument protection.

## Shares

Verify share class, voting and dividend rights, free float, controlling holders, treasury or quasi-treasury shares, dilution, depositary or custody structure, listing, liquidity, corporate actions, and governance. Separate market capitalization from enterprise value and accounting equity.

## Funds and structured instruments

Verify legal form, manager, strategy, benchmark, asset composition, fees, valuation, redemption or market-making, leverage, derivatives, counterparty and custody structure, tracking difference, and sanctions or settlement exposure. Do not assume the product label describes economic exposure.

For every instrument, record the source date and check for later amendments or corporate actions.
