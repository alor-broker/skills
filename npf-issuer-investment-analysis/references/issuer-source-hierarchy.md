# Issuer source hierarchy

For external verification, prefer in the order suited to the claim:

1. audited issuer financial statements and notes;
2. official issuer disclosures, prospectuses, offer documents, charter and corporate-action notices;
3. exchange and regulator records;
4. rating-agency rating actions and methodologies for the rating claim;
5. official sector, court, procurement, or register data where relevant and lawful;
6. management presentations and calls, identified as management claims;
7. broker research, commercial databases, aggregators, and news as secondary sources, subject to access rights and source identification.

Keep supplied internal analysis as a separately identified evidence class rather than presenting it as externally verified. For external claims, open the original document where possible and record publication and observation dates, accounting perimeter, language, units, and amendment status. A search snippet, cached card, or unsourced table is not enough. When sources conflict, preserve both claims, compare definitions and dates, and do not silently choose the more convenient figure.
