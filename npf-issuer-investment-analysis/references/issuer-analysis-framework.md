# Issuer analysis framework

## Business and perimeter

- Legal issuer, operating group, segments, geography, ownership, related parties, and consolidation perimeter.
- Revenue drivers, pricing, volume, cost structure, cyclicality, regulation, competition, and dependence on state policy or key counterparties.

## Financial quality

- Reconcile income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, equity changes, and notes.
- Separate recurring operations from one-offs, revaluations, FX effects, subsidies, asset sales, and accounting changes.
- Test operating cash conversion, working-capital movements, maintenance versus growth capex, capitalized costs, dividends, and related-party flows.
- Reconcile IFRS and RAS or explain why they are not comparable.

## Credit and resilience

- Gross and net debt with definitions; maturity schedule; secured, subordinated, guaranteed, and off-balance-sheet obligations supported by the available evidence.
- Interest burden, refinancing channels, covenant headroom where available, liquidity sources, asset encumbrance, ratings, outlooks, and event risks.
- Scenario sensitivity to rates, spreads, currency, commodities, demand, margins, regulation, sanctions, and market access.

## Valuation and falsification

Use more than one suitable method when inputs permit. Show formulas and assumptions. Define the conditions that would falsify the thesis and the indicators that would reveal them.
