# Conflict review

Use the evidence already in scope. Relevant indicators include common ownership, related-party transactions, organizer or market-maker roles, placement fees, cross-holdings, state or group relationships, remuneration arrangements, employee interests, use of non-public information, and enforcement findings.

For each indicator, record the source, date, relationship, potential incentive, affected conclusion, and an alternative explanation. Distinguish a disclosed relationship from a proven conflict and a potential conflict from misconduct.

When transaction-specific or internal conflict evidence is absent, state the unresolved dependency and the responsible compliance owner without prompting for disclosure. Absence of evidence is not evidence that a conflict does not exist.
