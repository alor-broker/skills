# Strategy framework

Use the evidence and assumptions already in scope. Mark each item by source and confidence, and expose missing decision dependencies without turning them into disclosure requests.

## Frame

- Subject: NPF sector, asset-manager, program, asset class, issuer, or portfolio in scope.
- Regime: pension savings or pension reserves. Within pension reserves, distinguish non-state pension provision from the long-term savings program and identify any program-specific public metric.
- Date and horizon: observation date, evidence cutoff, strategic horizon, and known data delay.
- Objective: preserve purchasing power, provide liquidity, limit downside, seek risk-adjusted return, or explain a trend. Use the stated objective; if absent, compare conditional objectives rather than inventing one.

## Compare options

For each general option, record:

- economic return driver and conditions required;
- exposure to rates, inflation, credit, currency, equities, commodities, and market liquidity;
- role in the liquidity ladder or duration profile under the stated assumptions;
- data quality and implementation dependencies;
- reversible and irreversible consequences in a hypothetical setting;
- strongest adverse scenario and indicator that would falsify the thesis.

When allocation ranges or target weights are part of the task, show the objective, constraints, data coverage, sensitivity, implementation assumptions, and approval owner. Distinguish an analytical recommendation from an authorized portfolio decision.

## Decision standard

A conclusion is supportable only when the evidence, metric definitions, dates, and calculations are reproducible and the strongest professional objection is either answered or carried forward as a decisive limitation.
