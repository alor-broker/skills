# Russian external source map

For external facts, use the source that owns the fact:

- Bank of Russia: key rate and monetary-policy communication, RUONIA, official exchange rates, zero-coupon government yield-curve data, financial-market statistics, NPF and asset-manager sector reviews, registers, and public regulatory material — https://www.cbr.ru/
- Rosstat: official inflation, national accounts, industrial, labor, price, and demographic statistics — https://rosstat.gov.ru/
- Ministry of Finance: federal budget, public debt, and OFZ auction information — https://minfin.gov.ru/
- Moscow Exchange: instrument data, trading calendars, index composition and methodology, market statistics, and government-bond curve methodology — https://www.moex.com/
- Issuers and rating agencies: issuer facts, financial statements, corporate actions, and rating opinions.

Record direct page or dataset URLs, publication and observation dates, units, methodology, revisions, and access date. Do not call a source official for a claim it does not own. Search snippets, aggregators, and news can locate a source but cannot replace it.
