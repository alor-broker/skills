# Market scenario framework

## Fact base

Record current policy rate, RUONIA and spreads, OFZ curve levels and shape, inflation level and momentum, public fiscal indicators, exchange rates, commodity indicators, credit spreads, equity indices, and liquidity measures. Use consistent dates or explain mismatches.

## Scenario construction

For each scenario state:

- trigger and time horizon;
- explicit shocks or paths, with units;
- assumptions about policy response, market access, settlement, and liquidity;
- transmission to rates, curve shape, inflation, currency, commodities, credit, equities, and valuation;
- sign ambiguities and second-order effects;
- observations that would falsify the scenario;
- refresh date or event.

Do not assign probabilities unless the method and evidence justify them. Do not assume historical correlations survive a structural break. When mapping a scenario to supplied exposures, show coverage, dependencies, and authorization boundaries separately from the analytical result.
