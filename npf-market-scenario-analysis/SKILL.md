# Russian Market Scenario Analysis for NPF Decisions

Analyze Russian macroeconomic and market evidence and translate it into scenarios for asset classes or supplied exposures. Cover monetary policy, RUONIA, the OFZ curve, inflation, fiscal policy, exchange rates, commodities, credit spreads, equities, and market liquidity.

## Work with the available context

Use market data, forecasts, exposure context, and research already supplied or obtained through authorized systems. Do not prompt for holdings, weights, trades, counterparties, liabilities, limits, mandates, committee views, internal research, account data, or credentials. If portfolio context is absent, provide asset-class implications and conditional mappings instead of requesting it.

Classify facts, market pricing, source forecasts, internal views, assumptions, and interpretation. Keep organization-specific details out of external searches and third-party tools unless the user explicitly authorizes the destination and purpose.

## Establish evidence and scenarios

Read [references/russian-source-map.md](references/russian-source-map.md) for external source selection and [references/market-scenario-framework.md](references/market-scenario-framework.md) for scenario construction.

Set observation and research cutoff dates. Prefer the Bank of Russia, Rosstat, Ministry of Finance, Moscow Exchange, original official releases, and identified supplied sources. Record release schedules, revisions, units, seasonal adjustment, methodology changes, and market-data delay.

Check whether a change is a level, rate of change, base effect, nominal or real, seasonally adjusted or raw, and already priced or genuinely new. Build a base narrative and materially different alternatives when useful. Trace transmission through rates, curve shape, inflation, currency, commodities, credit, equities, liquidity, valuation, and the exposures already in scope.

State sign ambiguities and second-order effects. Use ranges or sensitivities rather than unsupported point probabilities. Construct the strongest objection a macro strategist, rates trader, economist, or risk officer would make. Identify observations that would falsify each scenario and dates when it must be refreshed.

## Output

Return the cutoff date, fact table, market pricing, scenario assumptions, transmission map, exposure or asset-class implications, strongest objection, falsification indicators, refresh triggers, decision implications, and data limitations. Distinguish scenario analysis from order authorization, formal portfolio approval, and legal or regulatory conclusions. Respond in the user's language with equivalent safeguards.
