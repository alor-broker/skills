# Report quality checklist

## Identity and timing

- Source owner, document identity, and access classification.
- Publication or preparation, observation, reporting-period, and access dates.
- Later revision, restatement, erratum, or replacement.

## Scope and definition

- NPF, asset manager, issuer, sector aggregate, or market series.
- Pension savings or pension reserves; within pension reserves, non-state pension provision or the long-term savings program.
- Standalone or consolidated perimeter; RAS, OSBU, IFRS, regulatory, statistical, or management basis.
- Currency, units, scale, sign, gross or net basis, fees, taxes, accrued income, and valuation convention.

## Arithmetic and comparability

- Totals and subtotals reconcile within disclosed rounding.
- Ratios use the stated denominator and matching dates.
- Growth uses comparable periods and perimeters.
- Return, yield, duration, spread, and benchmark definitions are explicit.
- Missing, suppressed, not applicable, and zero are not conflated.

## Evidence status

- Audit or review status is described exactly as published.
- Footnotes, methodology, and limitations are included.
- Secondary summaries trace back to the original release.
- Sector aggregates are not treated as current organization-level facts.
- Internal and external figures are kept separate until their perimeters and definitions reconcile.
