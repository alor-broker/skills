# Source lineage

For each material figure, retain:

1. source owner, access classification, and location or stable identifier;
2. document or dataset title;
3. publication or preparation, observation, and access dates;
4. table, row, column, series, or page location;
5. original value, units, scale, and definition;
6. extraction or transcription method;
7. transformations, formula, joins, filters, currency conversion, annualization, and rounding;
8. resulting value and reconciliation check;
9. revision or replacement status;
10. limitation for the intended use.

A screenshot or search snippet is not sufficient lineage. Preserve enough source detail for an authorized reviewer to reproduce the number without copying restricted material into the decision record.
